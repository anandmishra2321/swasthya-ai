How to Use This Blueprint
This document captures the current feature vision in a structured hierarchy. Each role includes major features,
sub-features and detailed capabilities. It is a product-planning blueprint, not a claim that integrations or live data sources
already exist.
Role model and authentication principle
Users should not need to manually choose a role at every login. After secure email authentication, the backend
checks the account's approved category and affiliations, then opens the correct dashboard. A single person
may have multiple approved workspaces. Email text alone must never grant doctor, hospital or administrator
privileges.

PART B — CROSS-PLATFORM CAPABILITIES
These capabilities support multiple roles and keep the ecosystem consistent. They should be implemented as shared
services rather than duplicated independently in every dashboard.
1. Identity, Login & Account Security
Email authentication
  Verified email signup/login and recovery
  Automatic dashboard routing from approved account and affiliation records
  Secure sessions, logout, account recovery and suspicious-login monitoring
 
Workspace access
  One person may have multiple approved affiliations
  Switch between authorized workspaces after login when needed
  Server-side permission checks on every protected action
 
Account safety
  Least-privilege permissions, session expiry and access logs
  Credential verification for healthcare professionals and facilities
  Never trust an email string alone to grant a privileged dashboard
 
2. Location & Healthcare Discovery
Location handling
  Ask permission before GPS use
  Manual location/address search if permission is declined
  Use location only for the stated discovery purpose and provide controls
 
Directory quality
  Verified badges, source and last-updated timestamps
  Report incorrect location/contact/hours
  Clearly distinguish listed services from confirmed live availability
 
3. Real-Time Data & Integrations
Bed data
  Hospital system integration or authorized staff updates
  Timestamp, source, stale-data detection and reconciliation
  No fabricated real-time numbers; offer call-to-confirm
 
Doctor/clinic availability
  Calendar integration or provider-managed slots
  Prevent double booking where supported
  Update status on cancellation, leave or schedule changes

Key Dependencies & Reality Checks
  Real-time bed availability requires a hospital system integration or authorized staff updates, timestamps and a stale-data
 policy. GPS alone cannot reveal available beds.
 Real-time doctor slots require provider-managed schedules or calendar integration and safeguards against double booking.
  Medical-store stock requires inventory integration or timely staff updates. Stale stock must be labelled unknown or
 unverified.
 Ambulance dispatch and live tracking require an actual service-provider integration and operational support; a screen alone
 does not dispatch a vehicle.
 AI-generated clinical notes, report explanations and summaries must be reviewable and should not be presented as
 definitive diagnosis or autonomous prescribing.
 Professional credentials, facility licences, privacy, consent, record retention and emergency workflows require validation
 against applicable local rules before production deployment.
 Use synthetic/demo patient data for hackathon demonstrations unless there is explicit authorization to use real patient
 information.
Recommended End-to-End Demo Journey
  A patient signs in, grants location permission or searches manually, and finds nearby verified facilities.
  The patient compares hospital information, sees bed data with a clear timestamp, and calls to confirm before travelling.
  The patient books an available doctor slot or is registered by an authorized doctor/receptionist.
  The doctor reviews authorized history, records a consultation and creates a prescription.
  The patient receives appointment/medicine/follow-up reminders and can upload a lab report for an explainable summary.
  Hospital staff update bed status; the patient-facing directory reflects the latest authorized update and labels stale data
 clearly.
Product Definition
Swasthya AI is a proposed AI-enabled, location-aware healthcare coordination platform that connects patients
and verified healthcare providers, supports appointment and follow-up workflows, and presents availability
information with source and freshness transparency.
